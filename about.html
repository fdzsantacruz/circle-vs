About Circle VS

Circle VS is a free browser game where glowing circles armed with strange weapons fight inside a boxing ring until only one is left standing.

The idea is simple: pick a fighter, press Start and watch physics, weapons and a bit of luck decide the winner. It is made to be played in short sessions, with friends arguing over which circle is the best, or alone trying to beat a longer and longer streak against the AI.

The game runs directly in the browser on computers, tablets and phones. There is nothing to download and no account to create.

Who makes it

Circle VS is made by Propina Tech, an independent developer. The whole game, including the physics, the art, the sound effects and the AI rivals, is built from scratch with HTML5, JavaScript, Canvas and the Web Audio API.

The game keeps growing: new fighters, balance changes and new modes are added over time based on how people play and on the feedback players send.
