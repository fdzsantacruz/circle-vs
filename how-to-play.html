How to play Circle VS

Circle VS is a physics arena battler. Two circles bounce around a boxing ring, use their weapons on every chance they get, and the last one with HP left wins. You do not steer the fighters: your job is to choose the right circle and read the matchup.

Play now

The basics

  • Every circle starts with 100 HP (you can change this in Options).
  • The circles move on their own and bounce off the ropes. When they touch, or when a weapon is ready, damage happens.
  • Each fighter has one weapon with its own range, damage and cooldown. Some hit on contact, others shoot, throw or drag.
  • When a circle reaches 0 HP the fight ends with a knockout.
  • If a fight goes on for too long, sudden death speeds things up so every match has a winner.

Game modes

Fight

Pick two circles, write a name for each player and press Start. It is perfect for settling a debate with a friend: each of you chooses a fighter and you watch who was right.

Practice vs AI

Choose your circle and face AI rivals one after another. Every time you win, a new rival appears and the AI comes back with more HP, so each round is harder than the last. Your best streak is saved on your device, so you can always try to beat your own record.

The 9 fighters

Sword

A spinning blade that cuts on every touch. Simple, steady damage that rewards lots of contact.

Vampire

Bites, holds on and drains HP from the rival while it stays latched. Dangerous in long exchanges.

Spear

Throws its spear when the rival lines up. Strong at range, but it has to wait for the spear to recharge.

Rage

Taking damage charges a beast mode. Once it is full, Rage hits much harder for a few seconds.

Splitter

Launches a bouncing orb that halves the rival's HP. Devastating against fighters with a lot of health.

Harpoon

Fires a bouncing harpoon that hooks the rival and drags it closer, setting up constant small hits.

Electric

A short-range bolt that deals damage and delays the rival's powers, slowing down their weapon.

Shield

Blocks attacks that come from the front and throws its shield, which flies back like a boomerang.

Shotgun

A burst of pellets that is brutal up close and much weaker from far away.

You can also choose Random to get a surprise fighter every match.

Tips for a longer streak

  • Think about the matchup. A fighter that wins easily against one rival can struggle against another. Try different circles against the same AI pick.
  • High HP rivals: later AI rounds have more HP, so fighters that deal damage based on the rival's health, like Splitter, or that drain over time, like Vampire, become more valuable.
  • Range matters: Spear and Harpoon can hit from a distance, while Shotgun and Sword want to stay close.
  • Watch the cooldowns: many weapons have a short pause after they fire. The fights are often decided in the moments when one circle is reloading.

Options

In the Options menu you can turn sound and screen flashes on or off and change the combat balance: HP, speed, damage and cooldowns of every weapon. Create your own chaotic rules, or press "Defaults" to go back to the standard settings.

Controls

Circle VS is played with the mouse or by touch. Choose your circle, pick a mode and press Start. During a fight you can use the Pause, Sound and Menu buttons below the arena. It works on computers, tablets and phones, directly in the browser.
